# ជាងម៉ូតូ brand guide

## Brand idea

ជាងម៉ូតូ is the trusted neighborhood mechanic in your pocket: skilled, friendly,
fast, and familiar to Cambodian riders in both cities and the countryside.

The mascot combines four recognizable cues:

- A friendly Cambodian mechanic for human trust
- A wrench for repair expertise
- A motorcycle tire for the service category
- A restrained krama-inspired gold pattern for Cambodian character

Keep clear space around the logo equal to the width of the mechanic's wrench
handle. Do not stretch, recolor, add shadows, or place the wordmark over a busy
photo. Use the icon-only asset below approximately 160 px wide.

## Color system

| Role | Color | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary / trust | Mekong Navy | `#123478` |
| Dark background | Deep Navy | `#08152F` |
| Action / urgency | Cambodian Red | `#E31E32` |
| Cambodian accent | Warm Gold | `#F6B91A` |
| Light background | Warm White | `#FFF9F0` |
| Primary light-mode text | Charcoal | `#172033` |

Use navy for navigation and trust-building surfaces, red for primary actions and
urgent service states, and gold sparingly for highlights and cultural detail.

## Voice

Helpful, direct, respectful, and reassuring. Prefer everyday Khmer over technical
jargon. The brand should sound like a capable local mechanic explaining the next
step clearly.
